# Red Dragon

Create a blocky 3D dragon character built in a 2D pixel-art block style—so it exists in 3D space and can be rotated and viewed from 3D angles, while keeping every part strictly blocky, stepped, and pixelated like 2D pixel art turned into 3D blocks (no smooth curves or organic sculpting).

Dragon Appearance & Body Structure:

- Classic compact, slightly plump bird/chicken-like dragon body proportion that still looks bold and kingly.

- Clear head with horns/crown spikes and glowing eyes, a sturdy main body, two legs, two smaller arms/claws, and a tail extending from the back.

- Crisp blocky pixel/voxel-style edges with clean shading and highlights so it looks sharp and polished rather than rough or messy.

Animation & Fire Behavior:

- Smooth leg-movement walk animation where the two legs alternate cleanly while moving and plant steadily when standing still.

- Mouth opens and closes: when the mouth opens, emit small, subtle blocky pixel-style fire/sparks from the mouth; when the mouth closes, stop those small ambient flames.

- Triggering a big fire attack shoots a much larger, stronger blocky pixel-style flame burst/stream from the mouth in the direction the dragon is facing.
